Privacy Policy — Daylight Calculator

Last updated: [add date when you publish]

Daylight Calculator does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data with us — we don't operate a server at all. A small amount of information is stored locally on your own device to make the app more convenient; it never leaves your device except as described under Location below.

Location

If you choose to use "Use my location," your device's location is sent directly to two free, third-party services — Open-Meteo (for weather) and BigDataCloud (to confirm your city name) — solely to display sunrise, sunset, and weather information for that place. Your location is never sent to any server we operate (we don't operate any server), and never shared for advertising or any other purpose. You can decline location access and enter a location manually instead — every feature still works.

Stored on your device

The app saves the following locally on your device only, using standard browser storage (localStorage) — never transmitted anywhere, never accessible to us:

  • The last location you viewed, so reopening the app picks up where you left off instead of resetting
  • Any locations you choose to save as favorites (up to five)

Clearing your browser's site data for the app removes this information. Nothing here is an account or tied to your identity — it's just a convenience saved on your own device.

Other data

The app also sends the location you search for or type in to the same two services named above, for the same purpose (weather and place lookup).

No account is required. No analytics or tracking is used. No advertising is shown.

Third-party services used

  • Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) — weather data and place search
  • BigDataCloud (bigdatacloud.net) — reverse geocoding (coordinates to city name)

Each operates under its own privacy policy, which governs how they handle requests sent to them.
